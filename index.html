Physical parameters

      
Advanced Physics
All options below are implemented. Each can be configured independently. Defaults are circular and uniform. Coefficients are illustrative, not atmosphere fits.
Future / not implemented: ellipsoidal variations, reflection, Doppler beaming, spots, relativistic effects and interacting binaries.

      

      

      

      
Detached spherical stars; bolometric brightness scales as R²T⁴. Mass, radius and temperature are independent inputs. Phase zero is periastron (the +x direction for a circular orbit with zero periapsis angle). Inclination 0° is face-on and 90° is edge-on. Primary means the deeper instantaneous eclipse; equal-depth events have no unique primary.

    

    

      
Orbital plane
Barycentric motion
Cross: barycenter. Star 1: blue; star 2: amber. Radii and orbits share the same scale. Colors identify stars, not temperatures.

      
Observer view
Sky-plane projection
Positive z points toward the observer. The nearer disk is drawn on top. Disk colors are schematic; limb darkening is included in photometry when enabled.

      
Binary light curve
Out-of-eclipse flux = 1
Line and pink marker: noiseless flux, exposure averaged when enabled. Amber dots: fixed synthetic Gaussian observations when noise is enabled. Geometry and RV show the exposure midpoint.

      
Radial velocities
Star 1 blue · star 2 amber
Center-of-mass velocities; positive means receding. Systemic velocity is zero. No Rossiter–McLaughlin effect or exposure averaging of RV.
